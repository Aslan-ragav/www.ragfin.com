FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I contact RAGFIN?

You can use any WhatsApp button on this website to start an enquiry directly.

What services does RAGFIN provide?

RAGFIN currently provides assistance related to GSTR filing, Individual ITR filing, TDS filing and EPFO claim support.

What documents do I need?

Document requirements depend on the service and your individual circumstances. You can first contact RAGFIN to understand what is required.

Can I discuss the charges before proceeding?

Yes. Applicable charges can be discussed before proceeding with the requested service.

Is RAGFIN a government website?

No. RAGFIN is an independent assistance service and is not a government website or government department.

Important Information

RAGFIN is an independent assistance service. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or operated by the Government of India, GST authorities, Income Tax Department or EPFO. Government portals, statutory authorities and applicable laws remain the final source of official information and decisions.

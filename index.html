FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I request assistance?

Select a service and tap “Get Assistance”. WhatsApp will open with a ready-made message.

Do I need to visit an office?

The initial enquiry can be made directly through WhatsApp. Any document or verification requirements will be explained during the conversation.

What information should I share initially?

You can start by explaining the service you need. Avoid sharing sensitive documents or passwords until the applicable requirements have been explained.

Does RAGFIN guarantee approval or refunds?

No. Filing and claim outcomes depend on applicable government rules, submitted information, eligibility and processing authorities.

Is RAGFIN a government website?

No. RAGFIN is an independent assistance service and is not a government department or official government portal.

RAGFIN provides filing and claim assistance. This website does not represent a government department, EPFO, GST department or Income Tax Department. Service availability and outcomes are subject to applicable rules and verification.
